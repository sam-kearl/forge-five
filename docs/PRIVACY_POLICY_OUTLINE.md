# Privacy policy: draft outline

> **DRAFT. NOT FOR PUBLICATION.** This outline is a starting point for a qualified privacy professional. It is not legal advice and makes no claim of compliance with COPPA, GDPR/UK GDPR (including Article 8 and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code), CCPA/CPRA, or app-store rules. It describes the current mock-only build. It must be rewritten when a real ad or purchase provider is chosen.

1. **Who we are.** Publisher legal name, contact address and privacy contact email. *(To be decided.)*
2. **Who the app is for.** A general-audience puzzle game designed to be suitable for children. We treat every player with the most privacy-protective settings and do not attempt to determine age.
3. **Information we do not collect.** Names, emails, birthdates, precise or coarse location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, device advertising identifiers and persistent identifiers used for tracking. No accounts, chat or public profiles.
4. **Information stored on your device.** Settings, puzzle progress, statistics and the remove-ads status (see PRIVACY_INVENTORY.md). This stays on the device and is not sent to us.
5. **Purchases.** Processed by Apple or Google. We receive only confirmation that the remove-ads item was bought, and never payment details. The parental gate protects purchase screens.
6. **Advertising** *(complete only if ads are enabled)*. The provider's name. Non-personalised, contextual ads only. No behavioural advertising or remarketing. The child-directed / under-age treatment flags set. Exactly what the provider collects and why. How to remove ads.
7. **Analytics and crash reporting.** None *(update if that changes)*.
8. **Data retention and deletion.** On-device data is deleted with the app. Statistics can be reset in Settings.
9. **Children's privacy.** A statement of COPPA-aligned practices, parental rights, and how parents can contact us. *(Needs counsel.)*
10. **Regional rights.** EEA/UK, California and others, as applicable. *(Needs counsel.)*
11. **Security.** A short description.
12. **Changes to this policy.** How updates are communicated.
13. **Contact.**

Store listings must match this policy: the App Store privacy "nutrition label" and Google Play Data safety form (expected answer with the current build: *no data collected, no data shared*).
